Method

  • Past vs Future: Past = finished 2025–26 League Two homes with published attendance. Future = full 23-game League Two home slate for 2026–27.
  • No Premier League module: Gillingham are EFL League Two — this is not a Championship→PL promotion-jump model.
  • Sales data (League Two): Primary from Gillingham club published adult bands (£22–£25) plus EFL League Two comps. StubHub UK almost never lists L2 get-ins — secondary uses L2 sales priors (not Premier League resale).
  • Predictive model: GradientBoosting fill from Gillingham’s own League Two history by opponent tier; WTP from club/L2 sales priors. Confidence 0–100.
  • Ticket revenue = attendance × club sales primary band (~£22–£25).
  • Concession revenue (assumption): £6 per person (lower-league F&B placeholder). Same on both sides so lift is ticket / fill.
  • Optimization: PRICE_CAPTURE when relatively full and WTP ≫ face; PROMO_TARGET on soft gates (common at this level).
