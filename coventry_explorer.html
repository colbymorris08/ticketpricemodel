How to read this

  • Undervalued — secondary get-in is well above club face value. Fans are paying more on StubHub than the club charges. Action: HOLD inventory, raise category bands, package premium.
  • Soft demand — secondary near or below face value with weak listings. Action: PROMOTE (targeted offers), don’t blanket-discount hot games.
  • Optimized revenue — illustrative: lift average primary toward ~60–70% of observed secondary on HOLD games while keeping fill ≥ current. Not a club invoice.
