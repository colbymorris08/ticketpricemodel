Method

  • Past vs Future: Past = finished Championship homes with published attendance. Future = full 19-game Premier League home slate for 2026–27.
  • Championship → Premier League jump: Yes. Coventry has no PL home history in this sample. Secondary and fill for non-StubHub games come from a GradientBoosting model trained on other recently promoted clubs (Ipswich, Luton, Sunderland seed panel), blended with Coventry’s Championship fill baseline.
  • Predictive model: Features = big-six away (plus mild promoted-away bump). Live StubHub overrides the model when present. Confidence 0–100 reflects evidence quality (live market, seed analogs, days-out, domain-shift penalty).
  • Ticket revenue = attendance × average primary face value (estimated Championship/PL bands).
  • Concession revenue (assumption): £8 per person. Same on both sides so lift is ticket price / fill, not F&B.
  • Optimization: PRICE_CAPTURE when near-full and WTP ≫ face; PROMO_TARGET on soft games (targeted offers, not blast emails).
